# Language of the feature 001 evidence archive

The files under `evidence/` (143 tracked files, 117 of which contain German text as of 2026-11-01) are the qualification and
architecture-review records of feature 001 as they were produced: audits, review reports, measurement outputs and their frozen
inputs. Several are bound by digests in other records. They are preserved byte for byte and are not translated (constitution
principle VI, feature 011). The maintained feature documents beside this note (spec, plan, tasks, research, data model, quickstart,
contracts, checklists) are English and are the current reference; where they cite an evidence file, the citation refers to that
preserved record.
